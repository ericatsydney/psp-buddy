## What exists

- `src/psp_buddy/plan_act.py` `plan_and_act`: plan, open Move range if nothing is in reach, run to the forecast,

## Practical notes
- Create `local/runtime/ppsspp-v1.20.4/app/memstick/PSP/SCREENSHOT` before the first screenshot, and wait about 40 s after the
  helper reports ready: two PPSSPP access-violation crashes (c0000005) happened on a screenshot taken too early or without that folder.
  The helper stops with the emulator; saves were unaffected both times.
- Launch from the repo root: `.venv\Scripts\python local\move-attack-trial-20261017\navigation_handoff.py --config
  local\move-attack-trial-20261017\config.json --evidence-dir <new dir>`; the act declaration is `{"unit":"any","actions":["act"]}`.

## Open
- Hit chance is only known at the forecast, so the gate cannot compare it with a planned value unless the caller supplies `hits`.
- Damage stays on the fallback hit% x power; two logged attacks so far (one hit, one miss) are far short of the ~15 hits needed to fit a formula.
- The planner ignores the target's counter-attack (see the Z Gundam run); a counter-risk rule is the obvious next addition.
- Later milestones, not started: multi-unit loop, End Turn, enemy phase, retries and checkpoints.
